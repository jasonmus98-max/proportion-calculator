Welcome to Proportion Calculator (proportioncalc.us), a comprehensive mathematical utility platform designed to solve algebraic ratios, scale calculations, and geometric proportions with speed and precision.

🌟 Overview & Key Features
Proportion Calculator offers intuitive tools for students, educators, engineers, and everyday users who need instant, accurate ratio solutions:
Algebraic Proportions: Easily solve for unknown variables in direct and inverse proportion equations (a/b=c/x).
Scale & Ratio Adjustments: Scale models, architectural plans, and recipes up or down seamlessly.
Percentage & Ratio Conversions: Convert fractions, decimals, and ratios instantly.
Geometric & Dimensional Ratios: Solve 2D/3D visual proportions and scale factor adjustments.
